# Phoebe b-roll catalogue (Solid 45 s vertical ad)

Method: 1 frame/s at 540 px wide extracted to `research/frames/broll/<id>/f001.jpg...` (f001 is about t=0, fNNN is about t=NNN-1 s). Numbered 5x2 montages are in `research/frames/broll/<id>/m/`. Every frame of every clip was viewed in the montages; a handful of frames were re-viewed at 1080 px for screen legibility. Times below are seconds, accurate to about +/-0.5 s.

Common facts
- All clips are 1080x1920 HEVC with an AAC track. One talent throughout (long light-brown/blonde hair, grey ribbed halter dress, or pink blouse in 9fafb4). No second person's face appears in any clip.
- **7da362 and c13378 are the same clip.** Different file bytes, but SSIM 1.000 on video, same duration (20.2 s) and identical audio levels. Use one, delete or ignore the other.
- Audio: 57d321, 6a181d, 78ae24, ea6da6, fd4724 carry loud background music (mean -14 to -20 dB; the whisper text is lyrics: 78ae24 "stop the clock right here", ea6da6 "Maybe we're just a dream, a 2D fantasy"). That is third-party music; mute it in the edit. 4d9672, 0be329, 7da362/c13378, 9fafb4 are near silent (room tone, taps, laptop clicks). dd5b37 is digital silence (-91 dB). No clip has usable speech.
- The only laptop screen that ever shows legible content is 0be329. In every other clip the screen is small, blurred, faces away, or is a phone with the screen turned away.
- The only on-screen content is the Solid web app (solid.tech): "What can Solid help with?", prompt box "Describe a task...", chips "Reconcile Event Capacity / Clear Priority Prospects / Build Follow-Up Plan", "Not sure where to start? Book a call". Sidebar items and a right-hand panel with green bars are visible but too small to read. No LinkedIn, calendar, Slack, group chat or dashboard is visible anywhere. Those beats need screen recordings or recreations.
