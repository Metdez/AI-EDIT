Source: `Clicking through Solid footage.mp4`, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 223.7 s. Times are seconds. Frames sampled every 2 s, plus 1 s sheets around 0-12, 28-40, 84-96, 108-120.
Method note: I viewed the 112 frames as 2x2 tile sheets (4 frames per image) plus 1 s sheets, not as 112 single reads. Tile text is half-resolution, so anything small is "approximate".

## 1. What the product is and looks like

Solid is a chat-based AI agent workspace. Each agent is a named thread in a left sidebar. You type a task into one agent and it works for a long time, showing "Activity - N updates" and spinners. Agents also message each other. An agent can open a "Virtual computer", a cloud Chrome browser streamed into the app.

Layout (when the app window is shown wide, 1920x1080 coords):
- App window is a rounded white card at about x76-1842, y60-1018. It sits on a vertical-gradient teal/aqua backdrop, light aqua at top and saturated teal at the bottom (about #BFF1F3 top, #12D3E0 bottom). This is the screen recorder's backdrop, not the product.
- Left sidebar, x76-434: black hexagon logo plus "Solid" wordmark at the top-left (about x100-170, y80-100). Below it "+ New agent", "Resources", then a small caps "AGENTS" header, then the agent list, and "zack hanna" with a round avatar at the bottom-left (about y985).
- Centre chat column, about x620-1410 in most shots (x750-1530 in the later "Finance" and "Marketing" shots). Messages are left-aligned plain text. User messages are right-aligned light-grey pills. Suggestion chips sit above a rounded composer ("Message the agent...") with a paperclip and a round send button (black when active, grey when idle, black square = stop while running).
- Right "Links" panel, about x1600-1842: thumbnail cards ("Northeastern event co..." or "Virtual computer").
- Colours (approx.): app background #FAFAFA to #FFFFFF; sidebar selected row #EFEFF1; primary text near-black #111; secondary grey #888 to #9A9A9A (activity lines); user bubble #F0F0F1; composer border #E6E6E8; accent is essentially monochrome black. Small blue unread dots (#3B8FD9) mark agents. Backdrop teal #12D3E0. Title/outro card teal gradient #22D8E2, white text, pink/magenta logo mark #E6358F.
- Font: clean neo-grotesque sans (Inter-like). Dark text on near-white. The UI is very light and low-contrast, so it looks minimal and "Notion/ChatGPT-like".
- Spinners: small circular "C" loaders at the right of running agents. "Thinking..." with a sparkle icon. Streaming grey one-line activity items with icons ("Filtered Northeastern organizations for relevant technology...").

Agents listed in the sidebar (exact):
Marketing lead finder; Finance lead finder; Web portal sign-in; LinkedIn network creator research; Find SideShift lesson transcripts; Creator outreach campaign; Five-day AI engagement campaign; AWS sign-in; NEU Email Outreach; Ambassador outreach and scheduli... (truncated); StayLens real estate investment ...; Network creator research and N...; Community member demo outre...; Skool owner outreach campaign.
The order of the first two swaps when one starts working (Marketing is on top first; Finance moves on top after about t=28).

Bot names in the script (recruiter bot, CEO bot) do NOT appear. The visible work is lead research and outreach agents for a Northeastern University event, plus a web-portal sign-in agent.
