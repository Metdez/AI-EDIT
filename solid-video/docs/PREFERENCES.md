# Zack's preferences for short-form video edits

Collected from the brief and six feedback rounds on the Solid ad (20 Nov 2026).
How they were applied is in [PROCESS.md](PROCESS.md); the workflow that bakes them in is the
`short-form-video` skill (`.claude/skills/short-form-video/SKILL.md`).

## Before any editing

- Research first. Send several Sonnet agents to look at every file (video frames, audio, images) and
  write findings down before touching anything.
- Transcribe every audio source, including the reference.
- Study how the reference does things, plus the Nate and Motion videos' systems, and borrow from them.
- Work in a new folder of its own. Save all research to disk.

## The edit

- Copy the reference closely: fast cuts, same rhythm.
- Make many variants (ten here), not one.
- If the script covers something there is no footage for, build that footage (HyperFrames, three.js). Do not skip the beat.
- Use the brand logo (the Solid logo).

## The hook

- A sound effect on the hook that pulls people in, on every variant.
- A slight but visible zoom-in on the hook shot, on every variant.

## Sound

- Sound effects are required, and there should be a lot of them in the first 10–20 seconds.
- The first 5 seconds especially need a nice amount: an accent on nearly every stressed word (the Solid cuts carry 15 there).
- Background music under every video, modelled on the reference's track.
- Of the three beds made, the "bright" one (first used on variant 09) is the one Zack picked for all ten.

## Product demos

- No screen-recording crop floating in black space. The frame should be full.
- Where a clean crop-in is not possible, build an animated demo instead.
- If real screen footage of a site looks poor (the LinkedIn recording did), recreate the site as a built animation of browsing and clicking through it.

## Look

- Italic serif captions (first used on variant 05) look ugly. Use clean sans faces.
- No chapter/step pill at the top ("1/5 THE HOOK"). It is clutter.

## End card

- End on the brand's web address, not the bare logo: the Solid logo with ".tech" beside it, reading "Solid.tech".

## Wrap-up

- Put the finished videos in a folder of their own.
- Document the process and link the documents to each other.
- Say when the work is done.
- After a round of changes, update the skill and this file so the next session starts from them.
